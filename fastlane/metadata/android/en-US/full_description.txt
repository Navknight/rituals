Rituals is a habit tracker for people who want proof, not just a checkmark.

A day does not count until a photo is attached. That is the core rule: a tick
box is easy to lie to, a photo is not. Tap a ritual and the camera opens
instead of a checkbox. Photo proof is on by default for every new ritual, and
can be turned off per ritual for the handful of habits a photo cannot capture.

No account is required. Tap "Start tracking" and you are in, with a private
personal space created for you automatically. It holds your rituals, has one
member, and never shows invites, members or nudges. Connect a Google account
later from Settings and everything carries over, since the guest account is
upgraded in place rather than replaced.

Three kinds of ritual:
- Check: a single tap marks it done, for example making the bed
- Count: a daily target and unit, for example 8 glasses of water
- Timer: a daily target in minutes, for example 20 minutes of reading

Count and timer rituals accumulate through the day and show partial progress
in the ring.

Three kinds of schedule:
- Fixed weekdays, for example Monday, Wednesday, Friday
- A weekly quota, for example 3 times per week on any days you like
- Every N days, counting from the day the ritual was created

Skips, not misses. Swipe a ritual to skip a day. A skipped day is a
deliberate rest day: it holds your streak without extending it, and stays out
of your completion rate entirely. Rest days from the schedule work the same
way, so a Monday-only ritual does not lose its streak on Tuesday.

Habit score. Streaks are brittle, since one bad day erases months of
progress. Alongside the streak, every ritual has a score from 0 to 100%, an
exponential moving average that rewards consistency over perfection: keep a
daily ritual perfectly and the score reaches about 80% after a month and 96%
after two, while a missed day makes it dip rather than reset. The formula
accounts for each ritual's own schedule, so a three-times-a-week habit is not
judged against a daily one.

Stats and charts. Every ritual tracks its current and best streak, completion
rate over days it was due, total times logged, and a per-weekday breakdown
showing which day you actually keep it. Charts include a GitHub-style
heatmap of the last six months, a score trend line, a weekday bar chart, and a
month calendar, plus a Progress tab that rolls everything up across a space,
including perfect days in the last 30.

Optional shared spaces. Everything above works entirely alone. If you want
company, create a shared space from Settings and invite people by a
6-character code or QR scan. A shared space adds everyone's progress on a
ritual for today, nudges (a push notification to someone who has not logged
yet), and shared photo history. Personal spaces hide all of this.

Commentary with an attitude. The app has opinions about your progress, and
you choose how blunt they get. The tone setting runs from Off through Kind
and Dry to Brutal, with a separate swearing toggle that only appears on
Brutal and stays off until you turn it on. All lines are hand-written and
picked entirely on device: no model, no network call, and nothing the app
can say that isn't in its own source. Commentary shows up under the day
header, on the snackbar after logging, and on a ritual's detail screen when
there is something worth noting, such as a broken streak or a score worth
celebrating.

Rituals is free and open source software, built with Flutter.
