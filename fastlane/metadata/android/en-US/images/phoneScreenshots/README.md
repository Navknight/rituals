Screenshots go here, named `1.png`, `2.png`, ... in the order they should be
shown. IzzyOnDroid reads them straight from this directory.

They have to come off a real device or emulator, so they are not generated as
part of the build. Four or five is plenty: the home list with a couple of
streaks running, a ritual's detail calendar, the stats overview, and the
camera screen mid-proof.
