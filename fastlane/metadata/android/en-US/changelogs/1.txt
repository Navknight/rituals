First public build (0.1.0).

Early release. Nobody has used this on a real device yet, so expect rough
edges and please report anything that breaks.

- Track habits on your own, no account and no group needed
- A day counts once you attach a photo
- Check, count and timer habits
- Weekday, times-per-week and every-N-days schedules
- Skip days that hold a streak without extending it
- Streaks, consistency score, heatmap and charts
- Optional shared spaces with invite codes and nudges
- Light and dark themes
