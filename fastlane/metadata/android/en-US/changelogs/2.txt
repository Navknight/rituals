0.1.1

Fixes from the first real device testing.

- Opening a ritual sat on a spinner forever. The security rules for the new
  entries collection had never been deployed, so every read was denied and
  the app retried silently instead of saying so.
- Saving a photo failed with an authorisation error, for the same reason.
- Today and Progress could disagree about the same ritual, because a failed
  entries stream was drawn as "nothing logged" rather than as an error.
- Photos now come from the system camera app, so they get the phone's own
  processing instead of raw sensor output.
- Logged amounts can go down again. The sheet edits today's total, with
  plus, minus, quick steps and clear. Hold the plus button to open it.
- Timer rituals can be run in the app rather than timed elsewhere.
- Switching spaces had no effect on what was shown.
- The notification banner stayed after permission was granted.
- A solo log no longer calls itself sharing.
- Signing in with Google is the main option again. Guest was prominent
  enough that reinstalling and tapping it created a new empty identity,
  and the wording wrongly implied guest data survived a reinstall.
- Photos are kept on the device that took them, so a web user keeps their
  history even after the relay is pruned.
