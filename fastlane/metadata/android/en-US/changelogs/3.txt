0.1.2

A fix for photos going missing between the camera and the app.

- A photo taken could vanish on the way back from the camera. Android is
  free to kill the app while the system camera app is in front, and always
  does with "don't keep activities" turned on. The app came back on an empty
  screen and the photo landed nowhere. It is now remembered and claimed on
  the way back.
- Saving a photo closed one screen too many, taking the ritual that asked
  for the photo down with the preview.
