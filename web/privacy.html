Rituals privacy policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

What is stored

  • Account. A Firebase Authentication account: anonymous by default, or your Google account if you connect one. From Google the app keeps your display name and profile photo URL.
  • Your rituals and logs. Ritual names, schedules, targets and the days you logged, skipped or missed them.
  • Photos. Proof photos you take are uploaded to Firebase Storage so you, and members of a shared space you choose to join, can see them. A copy is also kept on your device.
  • Notification token. A Firebase Cloud Messaging token so reminders and nudges can reach your device.

How it is used

Only to run the app: show your rituals and stats, sync them across your devices, share progress inside spaces you join, and send reminders. Commentary is picked on your device; nothing is sent to an AI model. There are no ads, no analytics SDK, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties beyond Google Firebase, which hosts the data.

Who can see it

Your personal space is visible only to you. In a shared space, other members see your display name, photo, progress and proof photos for that space's rituals.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS and is protected by Firebase security rules that limit access to space members.

Deleting your data

You can delete a ritual and its history from the app at any time. To delete your whole account, open Settings → Delete account in the app. That removes your profile, your personal space with all its rituals, logs and photos, and your logs in shared spaces, then deletes the account itself. Your uploaded photos are deleted from storage at the same time. Nothing is kept afterwards.

If you no longer have the app installed, open an issue at github.com/Navknight/rituals/issues asking for deletion and it will be done within 30 days.

Children

Rituals is not directed at children under 13.
